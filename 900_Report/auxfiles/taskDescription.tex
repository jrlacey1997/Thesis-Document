Compare the RRT*-GBO algorithm with global solvers for constrained nonlinear trajectory optimization:

\begin{itemize}
	\item Perform a literature review of kinodynamic motion planning algorithms with applications in robotics and aerospace.
	\item Utilize, verify, and adapt the DLR designed RRT*-GBO algorithm.
	\item Implement several other State-of-the-Art (SOTA) global solvers from the literature.
	\item Apply the implemented algorithms to three systems to evaluate their performance: 1. an SO(3) spacecraft slew maneuver, 2. a 7 degree-of-freedom (DOF) robot manipulator, 3. an under-actuated aircraft (e.g. ducted fan).
	\item Implement dynamic (force and torque), kinematic (position, velocity, and acceleration), and spatial constraints (obstacle avoidance) for the evaluated systems.
	\item Evaluate and compare the performance of the RRT*-GBO algorithm and the implemented global solvers based on criteria such as computational efficiency, solution quality, and robustness.
	\item Document the findings and provide recommendations for the use of RRT*-GBO and global solvers in constrained nonlinear trajectory optimization for robotics and aerospace applications for future comparisons against machine learning-based approaches.
\end{itemize}
